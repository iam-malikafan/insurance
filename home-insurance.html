How Home Insurance Protects You

Home insurance steps in when something unexpected damages your home or your belongings – and when someone holds you responsible for an injury on your property.

  • Repairs or rebuilding after fire, wind, water and other insured events
  • Replacement of furniture, electronics, clothing and other belongings
  • Liability protection if a visitor is hurt at your home
  • Hotel and living costs if you can’t stay home during repairs

Do You Need It?

Ontario law

Home insurance isn’t legally required.

Your mortgage lender

Nearly every lender requires it before you can close on a home.

💡
Mortgage-free? Coverage is still worth it. A single fire or flood can cost far more than many years of premiums.

Core Coverage

1Dwelling

The structure itself – walls, roof, floors and built-in fixtures.

2Contents

Everything you own inside your home.

3Personal Liability

Injuries or damage to others that you’re legally responsible for.

4Additional Living Expenses

Temporary accommodation and extra costs while your home is repaired.

Extras Worth Considering

Sewer Backup

Water that backs up through your drains or sewer line.

Overland Water

Flooding from heavy rain, melting snow or overflowing rivers.

Earthquake

Damage to your home and belongings from an earthquake.

Valuables

Higher limits for jewellery, art, watches and collectibles.

Identity Theft

Help and expenses to recover your identity and credit.

Ways to Lower Your Premium

  • Bundle your home and car insurance
  • Choose a deductible that suits your budget
  • Install monitored alarms or water-leak sensors
  • Keep a claims-free history
  • Update your roof, plumbing or electrical systems
