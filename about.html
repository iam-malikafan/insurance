My approach

Insurance Advice That Puts You First

Insurance can feel confusing – long policies, fine print and prices that don’t seem to add up. My job is to cut through all of that, so you understand exactly what you’re paying for and why.

Working through Solutions Insurance Group, an independent Ontario brokerage, I’m not tied to a single insurer. That means I can compare several options and recommend the one that genuinely suits you.
