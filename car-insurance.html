The Basics

An auto policy is an agreement with an insurer: you pay a premium, and they step in financially when your car is damaged, stolen or involved in a collision. In Ontario, insuring any vehicle you drive on public roads is the law.

The right policy does more than keep you legal. It protects your savings if you’re found responsible for an accident, gets you back on the road faster, and helps cover medical and recovery costs.

What Every Ontario Policy Includes

1Liability

Pays if you’re responsible for injuring someone or damaging their property. The legal minimum is $200,000, but higher limits like $1–2 million are common and usually inexpensive to add.

2Accident Benefits

Helps with medical care, rehabilitation and support after a collision, no matter who caused it.

3Uninsured Automobile

Steps in if you’re hurt by a driver with no insurance, or in a hit-and-run.

4DCPD

Direct Compensation – Property Damage: your own insurer covers your car’s damage when another insured driver is at fault.

💡
Thinking about dropping DCPD? You’re allowed to, but you’d be paying for your own repairs even when someone else causes the crash. I’ll show you the real price difference so you can decide with confidence.

Popular Add-Ons

Collision

Repairs your car after a crash, whether or not you were at fault.

Comprehensive

Protects against theft, vandalism, fire, hail and falling objects.

Accident Forgiveness

Stops your first at-fault accident from pushing up your premium.

Rental Car Coverage

Keeps you moving with a rental while your car is in the shop.

New Car Replacement

Pays to replace a newer vehicle without deducting depreciation if it’s written off.

Enhanced Accident Benefits

Raise your benefit limits for extra security – I’ll explain what fits your situation.

What Affects Your Price

  • Your driving record and claims history
  • The make, model and age of your vehicle
  • Where you live and how far you drive each year
  • The coverage limits and deductibles you choose
  • Discounts such as bundling home and auto
